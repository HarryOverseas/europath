## Welcome to Harry Global Immigration

**Harry Global Immigration** (EuroPath) provides trusted guidance, educational pathway consulting, and relocation support for individuals and families aspiring to build their future in Germany and across Europe. 

Headquartered in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, with on-the-ground operational coordination in Germany, we bridge the gap between Indian ambition and European opportunity through authentic, transparent, and personalized advisory services.

---

## What We Offer

### 🎓 Higher Education & Student Visas
Navigating the German higher education system can be challenging. We guide prospective students through every stage:
- Initial profile evaluation and degree matching
- Public and private university shortlisting
- APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certification support
- Motivation letter, CV, and application review
- Blocked account setup and visa appointment preparation

### 💼 Opportunity Card (*Chancenkarte*) & Work Pathways
For skilled professionals seeking career opportunities in Germany:
- Comprehensive eligibility and points-based assessment
- Qualification equivalence checks (*Anerkennung*)
- German-standard CV and cover letter structuring
- Guidance on job-seeker and EU Blue Card pathways

### 🏥 Healthcare & Nurse Recruitment in Germany
Specialized pathway for qualified Indian nurses (GNM / B.Sc) aiming for careers in German healthcare institutions:
- Direct placement support with accredited hospitals and healthcare providers
- Structured German language learning up to B1/B2
- Nursing license recognition support
- Comprehensive relocation and family sponsorship guidance

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Reunion & Spouse Visas
Helping families reunite without administrative stress:
- Eligibility assessment and document checklist preparation
- Civil document translation and apostille/attestation guidance
- Essential German A1 language course enrolment
- Visa appointment scheduling and file preparation

### ✈️ Tourist & Visitor Visas
Explore the Schengen zone with confidence:
- Customized travel itinerary planning
- Financial document organization and insurance guidance
- Clear, compliant visa submission dossiers

## 🇩🇪 Harry German Language Academy

Language proficiency is the foundation of success in Germany. The **Harry German Language Academy** delivers focused, CEFR-aligned training led by experienced instructors:

| Level | Focus & Competency | Typical Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | Beginner fundamentals, everyday communication | Spouse & family reunion visas, tourist travel |
| **A2** | Elementary expressions, routine conversations | Enhanced daily integration, workplace basics |
| **B1** | Independent communication, professional terminology | Healthcare/nursing roles, vocational training (*Ausbildung*) |
| **B2** | Advanced professional & academic fluency | Nursing license recognition (*Anerkennung*), university degrees |

*Courses are offered with interactive sessions, authentic exam preparation (Goethe-Institut / Telc), and flexible batch timings.*

## 🛬 Germany Arrival & Settlement Support

We believe relocation support does not end when your visa is approved. We ensure every traveler lands with confidence:

- **Airport Pickup Coordination:** Reliable connection from arrival airports to your target city.
- **Accommodation Guidance:** Navigating student dormitories, shared apartments (*WGs*), and short-term rentals.
- **City Registration (*Anmeldung*):** Step-by-step assistance with mandatory local administrative registrations.
- **Essential Setups:** Support with local bank accounts, German health insurance, and SIM card activation.

## Our Values: Trust, Realism, and Transparency

- **No False Guarantees:** Visa issuance and university admissions are sovereign decisions made solely by respective consulates and academic institutions. We provide expert, compliant preparation—never deceptive promises.
- **Clear, Upfront Pricing:** Every service and proposed fee is explained clearly before engagement. Third-party statutory fees (consular charges, translations, insurance) are always itemized separately.
